# FluxGet Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

FluxGet is a free download manager and browser companion extension.

## Data Used by the Browser Extension

The FluxGet browser extension may access website URLs, page information, downloadable resources, and related browser data when necessary to detect downloads and send user-requested downloads to the FluxGet desktop application.

FluxGet may process website content and browsing-related information only when necessary to provide its download management features.

## Local Communication

The FluxGet browser extension communicates with the FluxGet desktop application running on the user's computer through a local connection (127.0.0.1).

Information required to process a download may be transferred from the browser extension to the locally installed FluxGet application.

## Cookies and Authentication

When necessary for a user-requested download, FluxGet may access authentication information such as website cookies so that the desktop application can download content that the user is authorized to access.

FluxGet does not use this information for advertising, tracking, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

## Data Sharing

FluxGet does not sell user data.

FluxGet does not transfer user data to third parties for advertising, marketing, creditworthiness, lending, or other purposes unrelated to the core functionality of FluxGet.

## Data Storage

Extension preferences may be stored locally in the browser.

FluxGet does not operate a service for collecting users' browsing history for advertising or profiling purposes.

## Purpose

Browser data accessed by FluxGet is used only to provide features requested by the user, including detecting downloadable resources and transferring downloads to the FluxGet desktop application.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated if FluxGet's functionality or data practices change.
